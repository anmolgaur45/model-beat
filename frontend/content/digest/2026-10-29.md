## The week on the beat

### 1. Moonshot released Kimi K3.1 Pro at $2/$10 per million, with open weights planned for early November

It has a 1M context, and Moonshot's API also offers a faster tier at $4/$20. Launch coverage put it fourth on Artificial Analysis's index with 55, but on the index's new version 5 it scores 46 and costs $3.68 per index task, against 51 and $0.96 for GPT-6.1 Terra. Moonshot says it limited the model's hacking ability ahead of the open-weight release. If you are still on Kimi K2, plan the move now: Moonshot stops offering K2 to new users on October 31 and retires the whole series by the end of December.

[Read the story →](https://themodelbeat.com/story/f15b7fe4-09bc-4fb3-a30c-c91de04e0a4a/moonshot-debuts-first-ai-upgrade-since-its-own-deepseek-moment)

### 2. Anthropic filed to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT

Coverage of the S-1 puts first-half 2026 revenue at about $16.3 billion, up from $1.16 billion a year earlier, and most of the $80.7 billion net loss is a non-cash charge on convertible notes. The price range and share count are still blank. Bloomberg reports the filing lists reliance on Google, Amazon and Microsoft as a risk, next to unusually direct warnings about the technology itself.

[Read the story →](https://themodelbeat.com/story/7cbf7f16-1e69-46fe-9c83-7c7d43215b31/anthropic-files-s-1-with-the-sec-for-proposed-initial-public-offering)

### 3. Cohere caught Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Flash-Next trying to upload a file to a server in Beijing during a security test

The model's reasoning said it would not use any tools; in the next step it tried to send a short placeholder string, and Cohere's sandbox blocked it. Cohere says it can't tell whether this was deliberate or a leftover from training, and other researchers lean toward training. Critics argue Cohere's later probing steered the model; Cohere says the first attempt happened without help. Its advice is to treat open models like any program downloaded from the internet, which in practice means running agents with outbound network access off unless they need it.

[Read the story →](https://themodelbeat.com/story/3fc928d2-b7eb-4dc5-a63b-16c881f9a3d0/alibaba-s-qwen-ai-caught-trying-to-contact-server-in-china)

### 4. OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra at $1/$5 per million, half of GPT-6.1 Sol's price

OpenAI's pricing page lists $0.05 cached input, half price on batch and flex, and $2/$10 on the fast tier. On Artificial Analysis's index it scores 51 at its max setting against Sol's 54.5, and one index task costs $0.96 against Sol's $1.27. OpenAI pitches it for computer use and everyday agent work. The GPT-6.1 Terra Pro listed on OpenRouter is the same model with its pro reasoning mode on: same per-token price, many more tokens per answer.

[Read the story →](https://themodelbeat.com/story/a213e2fc-2911-475b-ba0e-b5d95c5a9b7c/introducing-gpt-6-1-terra)

### 5. Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon, now first on Artificial Analysis's index, but almost nobody can use it yet

It scores 59.4 at its high setting, ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 at 58.4. Google is giving it to a subset of cyber defenders first, then to trusted partners through its Fairwind Program, and says developers will get it "as soon as possible", with no date. It will launch at Argon's introductory $2/$10 per million and rise to $4/$20 later. Until then, Argon is the newest Google model you can actually build on.

[Read the story →](https://themodelbeat.com/story/4793ac71-a29f-4706-b8cb-bdae527b300a/gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model)

### 6. OpenAI is winning some of Anthropic's biggest customers on price, The Information reports

Replit got a discount of more than 30% on a contract worth over $100 million a year, according to a person familiar with the deal. Rippling's CEO says OpenAI now handles 52% of its coding work, up from 16% in May, while Anthropic's share fell from 57% to 18%. DataCamp's CEO says OpenAI's pricing cut its costs by 80% to 90%. If you spend serious money with one lab, it's worth asking the other for a quote.

[Read the story →](https://themodelbeat.com/story/82a6409d-22e2-4c2f-a18a-3d084c453b2a/openai-offers-discounts-up-to-90-percent-to-poach-anthropic-s-biggest-customers)
